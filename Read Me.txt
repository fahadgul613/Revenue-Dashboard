# Revenue Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that visualizes revenue data across regions, years, product categories and colors. It uses pivot tables, pivot charts and slicers so you can explore the numbers in a few clicks.

## Features
- Slicers filter by Region, Year and Color
- Pie chart revenue share by region (Europe, North America, Pacific)
- Line chart monthly revenue trend from 2002 to 2004, split by category (Accessories, Bikes, Clothing, Components)
- Bar chart yearly revenue comparison by product category
- Column chart revenue by country (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, United Kingdom, United States)

## Key Insights
- North America contributes about 84% of total revenue
- Bikes are the top-selling category in every region and year
- The United States is the largest single market

## Dashboard Sheets
 Sheet  Description 
--------------------
 Cover  Main dashboard with all charts and slicers 
 pie by region  Revenue share by region 
 Line by month and year  Monthly trend 
 bar by year  Yearly category comparison 
 col by market  Country-wise revenue 
 Sample Data  Raw dataset 

## How to Use
1. Download the `.xlsx` file
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel (2013 or later)
3. Go to the Cover sheet
4. Click the slicers to filter the charts

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers
